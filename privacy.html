Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SignOn, Inc. ("SignOn," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use getsignon.com and our applications (the "Services").

1. Information we collect

We collect a minimal amount of information to operate the Services:

  • Account information: your email address and any profile details you provide.
  • Usage data: device type, operating system, and aggregate usage metrics.
  • Support requests: the content of any message you send to our team.

We do not read, store, or process the contents of your vault. All vault data is encrypted on your device using zero-knowledge encryption before transmission.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Services.
  • To send security and service notifications.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help operate the Services (such as our hosting and analytics providers), subject to confidentiality obligations, and where required by law.

4. Data retention & security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including TLS in transit and AES-256 at rest, to protect your information. We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@getsignon.com.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at privacy@getsignon.com or 100 Market Street, Suite 300, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA.
