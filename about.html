About

We're on a mission to make passwords a thing of the past.

SignOn was founded in 2019 by a small team of security engineers who were tired of choosing between convenient logins and real privacy.

Why we exist

Every year, hundreds of millions of people reuse the same handful of weak passwords across dozens of accounts. When one service is breached, attackers take the keys to the rest. Password managers were the first fix, but most are slow, closed, and make you trust the vendor with your secrets.

So we built SignOn differently: a zero-knowledge, end-to-end encrypted vault you can inspect, run, and audit — with single sign-on that makes the secure option the easy one.

Today more than two million people and thousands of teams rely on SignOn to protect the accounts that matter.
